Project 1: Getting to Know You

I made two people playing soccer. I have been playing soccer for almost my whole life, and it has played a big part in shaping me as a person. Soccer has helped me build friendships and create some of my favorite memories. It has also taught me how to work with other people and be part of a team. I love watching and playing many sports, but soccer has always been my favorite. I plan to play intramural soccer here at Tufts!
